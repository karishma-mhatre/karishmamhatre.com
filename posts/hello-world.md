I've been a Software Engineer for a while now, and one thing I keep hearing is: **write about what you learn**.

So here I am.

## What to expect

This blog is going to be a place where I jot down things I pick up - whether it's a tricky React pattern, a CSS trick that saved me hours, or something interesting I noticed while debugging.

Nothing polished, nothing formal. Just notes from someone who loves building things and is always learning.

## Why it matters (to me)

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain something simply, I probably don't understand it well enough yet. This blog is as much for me as it is for anyone reading.

If you're here, thanks for stopping by. I hope something I write is useful to you.
